## 🧠 Core Design Philosophies

1.  **Technology Changes; Humans Don't:** Tools evolve from T-squares to AI, but the psychological principles of how humans perceive information remain constant.
2.  **Form Follows Function, Always:** A beautiful interface that fails to solve the user's problem is art, not design. Design is problem-solving.
3.  **True Accessibility is Invisible:** Good design doesn't bolt on accessibility as an afterthought; it weaves it into the fabric of the product so seamlessly that it benefits everyone.
